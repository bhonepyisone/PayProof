# User Interview — PayProof

- **Who:** Shop owner (relative) — processes payment screenshots daily
- **When:** 2026-07-25
- **How:** Chat — ~15 min

## What they do today (without your project)

Saves every payment screenshot, carefully inspects it, then cross-checks against their bank app to verify funds actually arrived. Entirely manual process.

## What they liked

- **Automated checking** — PayProof taking over screenshot verification saves them a lot of manual work
- **API integration plans** — They liked the idea of embedding it directly into websites for a fully automated workflow

## What confused them / what's missing

- Even after PayProof verifies a screenshot, they still have to manually check the bank app to be 100% sure about transaction status. A bridge to handle bank-side verification would be valuable.

## What would make them actually use it

- Real-time continuous payment verification — instead of manually processing dozens of payments daily, an automated system that continuously verifies incoming payments would guarantee daily usage.

## What I'll change (next steps)

- [ ] Research bank-side verification bridge options for automatic transaction status confirmation
- [ ] Add real-time continuous payment monitoring feature to roadmap
- [ ] Prioritize API/webhook integration for website embedding
